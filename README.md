# KB Ops

Source for the KB Ops website: **https://kbops-portfolio.vercel.app**

KB Ops is my business operations consulting practice. I help Professional Services organizations and growing businesses improve the operational backbone behind delivery: resource and capacity planning, partner and vendor management, reporting and dashboards, workflows, systems, and automation (Airtable, Zapier).

## What's on the site

- **Services:** Professional Services Operations, and Business Systems & Automation
- **Ways to work together:** Operations Review & Roadmap, Operations System Build, Workflow Automation & Integrations
- **Selected work:** a global Professional Services organization, a wireless infrastructure company, a fleet management system, and a Shopify to Airtable integration
- **About:** 17 years in Professional Services operations, PMP-certified
